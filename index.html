Is this for you?

Let's save us both the call.

This is for you if…

  • You're posting short-form consistently and editing is the bottleneck.
  • You'd rather spend your hours filming and talking to your audience than in a timeline.
  • You want one editor who learns your style instead of a new freelancer every month.
  • You need short-form, long-form and thumbnails handled by the same person.

This isn't for you if…

  • You want the cheapest editor you can find rather than the right one.
  • You already have an in-house editor who knows your style cold.
  • You enjoy editing and have no intention of handing it off.
  • You're not filming yet — get some footage first, then come back.
